Your task:
1. Summarize only the most important facts from the document that affect the stock (revenue/earnings trends, margins, guidance, risks, valuation, catalysts).
2. Evaluate the strength of the bullish vs. bearish evidence.
3. Give a final recommendation: **BUY**, **SELL**, or **HOLD**.
4. Provide exactly 3 brief bullet reasons supporting your recommendation.
5. List the top 3 risks or red flags mentioned in the document.

Important rules:
- Do not use any external information beyond the provided document (unless I explicitly ask for market context).
- Do not give price targets or claim to predict the future.
- If the document is ambiguous or lacks enough data for a confident recommendation, state "HOLD" and explain why.
- Clearly state any assumptions you make.
- Keep the entire response under 220 words. Do not repeat facts across sections or add generic investing background.

Output format:
- markdown
- Executive Summary (1–2 sentences)
- Key Facts from the Document (up to 5 brief bullets)
- Bullish vs. Bearish Evidence (one brief sentence per side)
- Recommendation: BUY / SELL / HOLD
- Supporting Reasons (3 brief bullets)
- Top Risks (3 bullets)
